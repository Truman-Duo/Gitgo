Submit the independent review verdict and evidence through the existing review gate. Use findings only for concrete defects in the reviewed work; each needs a trigger, impact and inspectable location. Combine duplicate descriptions of the same failure and root cause. Put uncertainty, unexecuted tests and coverage limits in summary or evidence, not as informational findings. An empty findings list is valid. Submission is a proposal until the existing gate accepts it; this tool does not itself expand review scope or authority.
