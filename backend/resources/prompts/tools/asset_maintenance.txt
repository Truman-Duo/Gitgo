Manage a registered maintenance scope. Use wake(asset_id, instruction) to offer work, notify(asset_id, version, message) to queue a change for a subscriber, or asset_status to inspect current responsibility and versions.

1. Register a concrete workspace scope with its paths, decision and future commitment. To keep a subprocess responsible, select its process_id after its scoped result is accepted and promoted. Otherwise the main process holds responsibility.
2. For new work, give wake an instruction. The maintenance process accepts the work before editing. If wait_agents or list_agents returns asset_recovery_call, use that call to retry the current assignment from its retained scope. A separate generic child leaves the original obligation open.
3. For notify, put the update in message and identify its version. A notification grants no new work and keeps the accepted version. An already recorded delivery needs no duplicate notice.
4. Offer a transfer through the main process. The named successor accepts it against the current responsibility revision.
5. Revise an existing responsibility when evidence establishes a lasting change. Use revise with asset_id, expected_revision from asset_status, decision as the verified basis, and only the applicable add_paths, remove_paths or commitment. The responsible session stays the same and sees the revision on its next execution.
6. Retire an obsolete responsibility with asset_id, expected_revision and decision once required work, delivery and transfer are settled. The original asset remains available by its ID as history.
