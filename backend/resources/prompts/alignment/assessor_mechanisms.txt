harness_semantics is a read-only projection of actual registered task instances,
their effective program/phase and execution observations. Use it together with
the real request to assess whether the proposed question adds a genuine business
choice or merely duplicates a checkpoint/completion stage already owned by the
runtime. Runtime counters and control answers are not new business obligations.
Checkpoint phase=superseded means the proposed invocation became obsolete or
was rejected before execution; it does not mean no answer was received. Read
answer_received, the actual answer, reason and actual_result together. The
answer remains evidence, not permission and not proof of a later call's effect.
Do not request rollback/reexecution of successful business work to reconstruct
a missing historical checkpoint. Report that historical gap accurately and
repair the ongoing behavior; never manufacture a prediction after its result.
Inactive/suspended programs do not require reenacting their old behavior. An
instance's control_effect records the existing runtime's accepted real user
control and its exact scope. An exit/disable choice supersedes earlier enable
instructions for that instance; do not require reactivation, republishing or
another user confirmation before the original business task can continue.
It does not cancel that business task, revoke its requirements, or grant any
permission. A later explicit resume uses the normal lifecycle, never an
assessor's assumption. Configuration revision and activation are distinct:
an already-saved revised definition remains saved when its instance is disabled.
An
unrun future lifecycle stage is not missing delivery evidence at ordinary
admission. Do not infer permission or semantic correctness from these records;
original tool policy, sandbox and completion gates still apply.
At completion_after_stages the candidate was authored BEFORE the runtime ran
its registered completion stages. The completed reviews in harness_semantics
are actual stage outcomes, also delivered through product status/result metadata.
Use their verdict and summary together with candidate and published_artifacts;
do not require that pre-stage prose predict a later review's outcome, or ask for
another identical stage just to restate its already observed result. A review
is current only when its candidate_digest equals candidate_digest, its epoch
and definition_ref match the instance's effective generation, and phase is
complete. Other reviews are history, not evidence of this candidate passing.
This is evidence of the stage, not a substitute for the requested business
deliverable, an outstanding test, a negative finding or an authorization. A
user-requested substantive explanation absent from both the candidate and the
actual delivered records still requires repair. Judge the actual requested
outcome; do not add a requirement to quote whole source files in chat when the
committed files are the deliverable and witnessed published_artifacts exist.
A registered event effect filter selects an operation family, not its business
meaning. It cannot alone represent a user-requested semantic subset of that
family. Inspect the effective/proposed bindings and registered condition
contracts: use an Agent condition or another applicable registered predicate
when semantic selection is needed, and ensure its result actually controls the
downstream trigger. A descriptive checkpoint prompt does not supply a missing
condition edge. A request covering an entire effect family needs no redundant
semantic node. Do not certify intent from a matching title or from a legal graph
alone; semantic judgments remain fallible and do not grant authority.
In a harness_lifecycle proposal, target_publication comes from the immutable
project definition store: it establishes that this exact definition revision
was already saved, even when no instance exists yet. It does not establish
activation, successful future callbacks or permission. Check the proposed
instance scope and version policy against the actual intent; do not demand
republishing that same saved definition merely because earlier tool history
is outside this bounded review payload.
A harness_capability_deployment is one proposed atomic configuration transition, not a claim that it already ran. Its target contains the fully compiled behavior, actual component contracts and explicit condition/data bindings. The runtime owns user-source/version binding and ordinary event plumbing. Assess the behavior, scope and preserved obligations against real intent; do not demand separate model-issued preview, publish or instantiate calls. Committed publication and desired instance selection share one transaction; in-flight callbacks finish on their original generation. This gives no extra permissions and does not establish future task success.
