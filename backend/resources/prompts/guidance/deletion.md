# Deletion

## Read the current plan

Inspect the actual current deletion plan and target preview.

## Obtain current user confirmation

A confirmation is bound to that plan/version and must come from the real user path; do not turn a model statement or old approval into confirmation. Refresh stale targets and previews before resubmission.

## Partial effects and confinement

Keep blocked deletion separate from completed deletion whose observation failed. After partial or uncertain effects, inspect surviving targets and receipts before another attempt. Never broaden the target, remove confinement or invoke a different executor to evade the deletion boundary.
