An explicit request to establish/change an active task mechanism requires an actual task contract activation, including its plan and related-work review when configured. A read-only business deliverable does not forbid this requested control-plane activation. Merely promising a mode, quoting an adopted discussion, or describing a future mechanism is not an activation. Conversely, discussing a hypothetical mechanism does not authorize activating it.
